## Projects (6)

### 1. Tansiq Simulator
- **Title:** Tansiq Simulator
- **Description:** Full-stack Egyptian Tansiq placement simulator — students register with a certificate type, track duration, and total score, then fill 75 numbered choice slots with instant autosave. Admins manage the yearly cutoff-score dataset through a dedicated panel.
- **Tech Stack:** Next.js 16, NextAuth v5, Prisma, PostgreSQL, next-intl
- **Features:** Student registration, 75-slot choice grid, instant autosave, admin cutoff panel, i18n (AR/EN)
- **GitHub:** https://github.com/ahmadabdallahh/tansiq-simulator
- **Featured:** Yes
